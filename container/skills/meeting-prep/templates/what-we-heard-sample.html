CMT is pursuing a firm-wide initiative to scale business development operations through AI-enabled automation. The Pursuits Group, led by Konner Dudley, is looking to double proposal output within existing team capacity — transforming how proposals, qualifications, and pursuit assets are generated across 25+ offices and five market verticals.

What We Heard

During our conversation, you shared several key challenges shaping your current priorities:

Priority 1
Proposal Generation at Scale

Your team needs to 2x proposal output within the same hours. Current assets live in SharePoint and are queryable via Copilot, but results are read-only — requiring manual copy/paste into new documents. There's no generative layer, no templating engine, and no structured output pipeline to accelerate production.

Priority 2
The Governance Balance

You're navigating between two failure modes: a centralized model where your marketing team becomes the bottleneck for all doc generation (doesn't scale), and a decentralized model where 25+ offices generate content independently (inconsistent quality, no brand governance). The goal is a governed self-serve layer — guardrails your team controls, outputs the field can generate.

Priority 3
Resume & Project Sheet Pipeline

The path from Deltek Vantage to usable proposal-ready content is broken. Prior approaches — including a custom build that was ultimately retired, and manual AI-assisted workflows — got closer but still can't produce structured, brand-compliant output at scale. Extracting usable content from legacy PDF assets remains the core unsolved problem.

Priority 4
Pursuit Intake & Qualification

The workflow from opportunity identification through Deltek Vantage intake to Monday.com pipeline management is largely manual. Go/no-go decisions and pursuit kickoff processes present clear automation opportunities, including AI-powered RFP qualification and overnight opportunity monitoring.

Your Goals

  • 40% automation target across pursuit operations — a specific, internally validated benchmark your team is working toward
  • 2x proposal output in the same hours — the primary OKR driving this initiative
  • Brand governance at scale — enabling field teams to generate compliant content without centralized bottlenecks
  • Integration with existing stack — any solution must work within the Microsoft / Deltek / SharePoint ecosystem, not replace it

Suggested Approach

Based on what you shared, we see two paths forward — and they're not mutually exclusive:

Recommended Starting Point
Constraint Sprint

Pick one constraint — proposal generation — and scope a standalone proof-of-concept. Define a clear success metric (e.g., "generate a compliant first-draft SOQ from existing SharePoint assets in under 15 minutes"). Validate in 3-4 weeks, then scale into the broader ecosystem.

Full Scope Option
AI Enablement Roadmap

Map the full integration architecture across all five pain points — Deltek, SharePoint, OpenAsset, Monday.com — define the governance model, and deliver a phased implementation plan with clear decision gates at each stage.

Our recommendation: start with the sprint. It directly addresses your #1 priority, proves the model works with real output, and naturally scopes the larger roadmap as you validate the approach. You earn confidence in the partnership by shipping something that works first.
